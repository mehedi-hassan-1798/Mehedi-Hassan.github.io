Instructor, EECS · Ph.D. in Computer Science

Understanding changing networks.
Detecting emerging threats.

I work at the intersection of cybersecurity, machine learning, and network science. My research develops methods to detect anomalous links and suspicious communication patterns in dynamically evolving networks.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Louisiana at Lafayette in 2024, advised by Dr. Mehmet Engin Tozal. At Arkansas, I teach programming, data science, and AI for security.
